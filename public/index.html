PayOnProof Accounts payable for construction subcontractors
Sandbox: no real money

Pays subcontractors only for work it can prove.

An AI reads the invoice, the site manager's timesheets and the delivery notes. Fixed rules prove every line against them and the contract rates. You approve the exact amount, PayPal pays it, and the payout is read back from PayPal as proof.
